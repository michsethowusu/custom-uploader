LEIN Speech Dataset — Station Audio Upload

Share recordings of Liberian English / Kolokwa speech. Files or entire folders are sent in small resumable chunks and published directly to the public Hugging Face dataset. You can pause, lose connection, or close this tab — uploads pick up where they stopped.

Recording details

By uploading, you confirm you have permission to share these files publicly under CC BY 4.0.

Select audio files or folders

Drop files or a whole folder, or use browse folders for a large folder with subfolders — the folder structure is preserved in the dataset. Accepted: WAV, FLAC, MP3, M4A, AAC, OGG, Opus, WebM, ZIP, CSV, JSON, TXT. Up to 5 GB per file.
